# Assistant UX: user feedback and implications

Research date: 2026-09-05. Companion to [the implementation proposal](whatsapp-personal-assistant.md).

## Method and limits

Read live X search results in the signed-in browser for Grok Bot, Town, Poke, OpenClaw 2 and Hermes, with recent-date filters. Refined ambiguous Town/Poke searches to their product handles. Excluded unrelated name matches, generic Grok-chatbot complaints, pure announcements and unsupported earnings claims from conclusions. Affiliations of individual posters were not independently established. This is a qualitative convenience sample, not representative sentiment, a product ranking, or measured reliability.

Read Grok Bot and Town US App Store pages plus Apple's recent-review feeds. The feed returned 50 Grok records, 5 Town records and 19 OpenClaw records; the displayed sample inspected was the first 15 Grok, all 5 Town and the first 15 OpenClaw, plus featured page reviews. Counts describe retrieval, not endorsement or unique people across surfaces. Most OpenClaw reviews predate the August 30 release and must not be called reviews of 2.0. No official Poke listing was verified. Hermes mobile listings found included third-party clients; their ratings are not Nous Hermes ratings. No apps were installed or runtime-tested.

## Synthesis

The strongest convergence is a dependable assistant that owns follow-through: easy capture, work continuing away from the device, useful output, and a clear way to intervene. There is evidence for both one familiar assistant and understandable specialization. There is no basis in this sample for making several personalities a prerequisite.

For Expert, adopt one default assistant, distinct task records, a phone-accessible computer, versioned behavior that actually reaches the next turn, and delegated coding with compact evidence. Preserve existing bots; do not delete them to simplify onboarding.

WhatsApp is a strong distribution and habit surface, not a unique feature. [Poke](https://poke.com/) explicitly includes it, and [Town pricing](https://www.town.com/pricing?audience=teams) also lists WhatsApp. Expert's differentiator to test is the complete combination, not any single checkbox.

- [Claude Cowork surfaces](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15520349-use-claude-cowork-on-web-desktop-and-mobile): continuity and explicit distinctions between cloud work and desktop-dependent capabilities.
