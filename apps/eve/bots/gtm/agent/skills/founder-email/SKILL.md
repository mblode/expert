# Founder email

It goes out under the human's name, so read `/workspace/voice.md` first.
It reads as one person writing to one person or it does not get opened
twice.

## Shape

Five sentences, rarely more.

1. **Why them, specifically.** Something true about that person or
   company that you verified, not a merge field. If you cannot find one,
   this is the wrong recipient.
2. **What you built**, in their words, one sentence.
3. **The one piece of proof** from `proof.md` that makes it credible.
4. **The ask**, small and specific. A reply, a look, fifteen minutes. Not
   a demo, not a call to "explore synergies".
5. **A way out.** One line that makes ignoring it easy.

Subject line: what it is about, lower case, no colon-and-clever, never a
fake "re:".

## Never

- No flattery that could be pasted into any other email.
- No "quick question" when there is no question.
- No made-up mutual connection, event, or shared interest.
- No attachment, no calendar link in a first email.
- No follow-up that says "bumping this to the top of your inbox".

## Output

Draft to `/workspace/gtm/campaigns/<slug>/<recipient>.md` and show the
body in the message. Mark every `[?]` you could not fill. The human
sends, or tells you to in that turn.
