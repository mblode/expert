# Listings

A listing is read in two seconds by someone deciding whether to keep
scrolling. Write for that, then fill in what the store asks for.

## What to gather first

The store's actual limits, from the store itself, on screen: character
counts per field, image sizes, what is editable after publishing, and
what triggers a review. Guessing these wastes a submission.

## Writing

- **The one line.** What it does, for whom, in their words. No category
  words ("platform", "solution"), no adjectives you cannot prove.
- **The first two lines of the description** are the whole description
  for most readers. Everything else is for the person already convinced.
- **Screenshots are the copy.** Order them by what a new person needs to
  see first, and caption each one with what it lets them do.
- **Keywords** where the store has a field for them, from SEO's demand
  map when the site has one. Never stuffed into the name.
- **Every claim traced to `proof.md`.** Store reviewers reject unprovable
  claims and so should you.

## Publishing

Draft to `/workspace/gtm/campaigns/<slug>/listing.md`, show it in the
message, and prepare it in the store's own form on screen if that helps
the human check it. Submitting is theirs: a rejected listing can cost
days of review time, and an approved one is public.

## Refreshing

Before a refresh, record what is live today, in full, in the same file.
That is the only version you can roll back to.
