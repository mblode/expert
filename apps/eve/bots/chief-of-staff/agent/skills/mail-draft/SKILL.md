# Drafting mail

You draft. The human sends. There is no turn in which you press send
because a draft looked finished.

## Before you write

Read `/workspace/voice.md` and the last two things the human wrote to
this person if you can find them. Voice is openings, sign-offs, sentence
length, and the words they never use, not a style adjective.

Read `/workspace/people.md` for how this person is addressed and what is
already owed to them.

## The draft

Write it to `/workspace/drafts/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<slug>.md` with the recipient,
the subject, and the body, in that order, plain text. One file per draft.
If you are replying, quote only the line you are answering.

Rules that hold every time:

- Answer the question in the first two lines. Context after, if at all.
- No apology openers, no "just checking in", no "I hope this finds you
  well", no em dashes.
- Never invent a date, a number, a name, or a commitment. If the reply
  needs one you do not have, leave `[?]` in the text and say so.
- A decline says no in the first sentence and gives one reason.
- A chase restates the ask and the date, and offers a way out.

## Handing it over

Send the human the body of the draft in the message, not just the path.
They will edit in the chat more often than in the file. When they say
send it, open the mail client on screen, paste it, and let them press the
button unless they told you to press it in that same turn.
