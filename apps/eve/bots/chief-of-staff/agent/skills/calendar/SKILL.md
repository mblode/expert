# Calendar

The calendar lives behind a sign-in in the browser on my screen, so this
is screen work: read `skills/screen` first if you have not this session.

## Reading

Open the week view, screenshot, and read it. Zoom rather than guess at a
small label. Report in the human's timezone (Australia/Melbourne) and say
the day, not the date, for anything inside the next week.

What matters in a read: what needs preparation, what is double booked,
what is a commitment to another person, and what has no agenda.

## Holding time

Holding a block on the human's own calendar is fine when they asked for
focus time or you are protecting preparation for something already
booked. Name the block for the work, not for yourself.

Anything that invites another person, moves a meeting someone else owns,
or declines on the human's behalf waits for them to say so in that turn.
Draft the message instead and say the draft is ready.

## Preparing a meeting

One file in `/workspace/brief/` per meeting worth preparing: who is in
the room, what they last talked about, what the human wants out of it,
and the two questions they should ask. Then send them the two questions.
Nobody reads a prep document on the way to a meeting.
