# Brand

`/workspace/design/brand.md` is the source. Read it before drawing
anything, and change it deliberately rather than in passing.

## What the file holds

- **The mark**: what it is, what it must never be put next to, its
  minimum size, and its one-colour version.
- **Type**: two families at most, the scale (a ratio, not a list of
  arbitrary sizes), and the weights actually used.
- **Colour**: a small set with names and roles, each with the contrast
  pairs that are allowed. A palette without roles becomes decoration
  within a month.
- **Spacing and radius**: one scale, used everywhere.
- **Voice**: how the product speaks, with three sentences it would write
  and three it never would.

## Changing it

A brand change is a decision, not a preference. Show the current and the
proposed side by side on the real product, not on a swatch: a palette
that looks good in a grid can be unreadable on a form.

Write what changed and what it replaces to
`/workspace/design/decisions.md`, dated. Then say, in the message, every
place that now needs updating: the product, the site, the store listings,
the mail templates. A brand changed in one place and not the others looks
like a mistake rather than a decision.

## Applying it

Use the token names, never raw values. When a design needs something the
brand does not have, that is a brand question first: either the brand
grows by one deliberate entry, or the design uses what exists. Never a
quiet one-off hex.
