# Writer briefs

The brief is the deliverable. It is finished when a competent writer who
does not know this product could write the page from it and get it right.

Write it to `/workspace/seo/briefs/<slug>.md`:

- **Query and intent.** The primary query in the searcher's words, the
  intent, and the two or three secondary queries the page also answers.
- **The reader.** Who they are, what they already know, what they are
  trying to do next.
- **The promise.** One sentence: what this page gives them that the top
  results do not.
- **Shape.** The sections in order, each with a line on what belongs in
  it. Where the answer to the primary query sits, which should be near
  the top.
- **What must be true.** The facts, numbers and product details the
  writer must not get wrong, with sources. Anything you are unsure of
  goes in as a question, not as a fact.
- **Evidence to include.** The screenshot, the example, the data point.
  A page with something nobody else has is the whole difference.
- **Internal links.** Which existing pages link in, which this links out
  to.
- **Title and meta description** as suggestions, marked as suggestions.
- **What not to do.** The angles competitors took that did not work, and
  anything that would be a claim the product cannot support.

## Rules

- No word count target. Length follows the shape.
- No keyword density, ever.
- Never draft the prose "to save them time". A brief with the article
  attached is an article, and it will be published as one.
- One brief per page. A brief covering three pages is three briefs.
